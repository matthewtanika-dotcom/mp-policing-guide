Privacy Policy

MP Policing Guide

Last updated: 17 August 2026

Privacy

MP Policing Guide is an offline reference application designed to provide policing guidance and reference information.

The app does not require users to create an account and does not collect, store or transmit personal information.

Information collected

MP Policing Guide does not collect names, email addresses, telephone numbers, precise location data, photographs, contacts or other personal information.

Analytics and advertising

The app does not contain advertising and does not use third-party advertising or analytics services.

External services

The application is designed to function offline. No personal information is transmitted to external services by the app.

Changes to this policy

This privacy policy may be updated if the functionality of the application changes.

Contact

For questions regarding this privacy policy, please use the contact information provided on the MP Policing Guide website.
