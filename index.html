    Arrest & custody
    Power to arrest: section 67 AFA 2006 is the main service power of arrest for a service offence. Necessity / procedural requirements: SPCoP Code G (arrest) and Code C (detention, treatment and questioning) govern how that power must be used. The two are kept separate below. Wording here is a plain-language prompt, not the verbatim legal caution — confirm exact current wording in Code C before using it operationally.
    Power to arrest — necessity
    Before you arrest
      Necessity / procedural requirements
      At the point of arrest — legally required disclosures
      Under Code G, at the time of arrest (or as soon as practicable after) you must make clear to the person: the fact that they are under arrest, and the grounds and reason for the arrest, even if these seem obvious. Failure to do this may make the arrest unlawful.
        On arrival at service custody — entitlements to explain
          Time limits and reviews
          Section 98 AFA 2006: a person arrested under section 67 must not be kept in service custody without charge except in accordance with the further provisions of the Act. Confirm current review periods and authorisation levels with your custody officer/SOPs.
