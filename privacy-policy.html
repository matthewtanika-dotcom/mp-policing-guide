MP Policing Guide – Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 November 2026

MP Policing Guide is an unofficial offline reference and training aid for UK Service Police. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the MOD, RMP, RNP, RAFP or the Defence Serious Crime Command.

Data we collect

None. The app has no accounts, no adverts and no analytics, and the developer does not collect, store or receive any personal data.

Data stored on your device

Your settings, favourites, recently viewed items and any notes you type are stored only on your device. They are never sent to the developer. Clearing the app's data or uninstalling the app removes them.

Network use

The app works offline. If your device is online, the app may check GitHub's public release information to tell you whether a newer version exists. That request contains no personal information from the app.

Microphone and speech

Where the app offers voice features, they use your device's own speech services, which are provided by your device manufacturer or Google and are governed by their privacy policies. The app does not record or keep audio.

Children

The app is intended for adults working in or studying Service Police roles.
